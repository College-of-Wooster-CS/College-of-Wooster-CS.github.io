Development Lab / CS Help

To enter Taylor after 5 pm, you will need to use the North entrance and swipe your ID card.

  • Taylor STEM Zone - Taylor 205

    Open Sunday - Thursday 7-9pm, and Wednesday & Thursday 2-4pm.

    Zone Interns (ZIs) & teaching assistants (TAs) provide assistance for 100-level courses (100, 102, 112, 120). No appointment necessary. They are able to assist you with any introductory computer science course (or any other STEM course taken in Taylor).

  • Development Lab: VR Equipment & High-Performance Computers - Taylor 204

    Open Tuesday & Thursday 4-5pm with assistant Leslie Osei-Anane.

    The development lab and assistant can support you with projects requiring VR equipment (headsets and/or treadmill) or high-performance computers. Note that the room, Taylor 204, is accessible through Taylor 200.
